## Goal
Test whether a **PLAYBOOK/skills/connectors refactor** (thin always-on router + progressive-disclosure
recipes) + two capability fixes (**L1** name-resolution, **L2** "where"→map) improve **answers, speed,

datasets**, not just citing titles.

**conservation of rarer species**; **taxonomy** of these species. Papers-first, points-in-datasets.

   `agents/hermes/TRACE_INTROSPECTION.md`).
