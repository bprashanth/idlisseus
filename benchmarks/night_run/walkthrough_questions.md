# Walk-through questions for the EBTL team (the DSS hook)

Provocative, data-backed questions to open a conversation — each is something the platform *found* that the
team may not know, framed to invite them in. (Harvested from the overnight run; sources in each answer's /why.)

## Water — the sharpest hook
- **"Did you know most of your ponds hold water only ~1 month a year?"** JRC 38-yr surface-water: **6 of 11
  waterbodies are ephemeral (~1 month/yr)**, and the **NE pond is the most precarious** (3% occurrence,
  cropland setting → evaporation + irrigation draw). *Caveat: JRC 30 m misses sub-30 m farm/check-dams —
  which is exactly why we'd want their pond GPS.* → natural ask: "send us your pond waypoints."

## Invasives
- **"Lantana here seems to invade mainly under teak canopy gaps."** Its strongest native associates by
  presence-proximity are **Lagerstroemia parviflora** and **Vitex altissima** (shared dry-deciduous habitat).
- **"Nobody has actually logged Prosopis or Parthenium at EBTL"** on GBIF/iNaturalist — the site's invasive
  diversity is under-sampled. The maps are *transfers* from regional records, not observations → "help us
  ground-truth: which invasives do you actually see?"

## Elephants
- **"Elephants are active in your western corridor — 16 records in 2024–26."** Available forage skews to
  **bamboo shoots/stems and Grewia browse**. But: this is *available food + opportunistic sightings*, not
  diet or systematic movement → the honest ask is **camera traps (3 months) + dung transects**.
- **"There are no protected-area boundaries (WDPA) over your elephant corridor"** — so formally it's
  unprotected connectivity; worth flagging for the Forest Department.

## The meta-hook (why a DSS, not a chatbot)
Every answer above **names its sources, states its limit, and asks for the ONE dataset that would sharpen
it** (pond GPS, camera traps, plot lists, boundary files). That loop — *honest estimate now → concrete data
ask → sharper answer next* — is the platform's value: it turns "we don't have data" into a prioritised
data-collection plan.
