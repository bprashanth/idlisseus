"Community-based fire management (CBFM) programs in the Nilgiris have demonstrated measurable reductions in annual burned area when villages participate in joint monitoring and controlled burning schedules. Unlike top-down suppression strategies, CBFM leverages traditional ecological knowledge held by local Toda and Irula communities, integrating seasonal fire calendars with modern remote-sensing alerts.
