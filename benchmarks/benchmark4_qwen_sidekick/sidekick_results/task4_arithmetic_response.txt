To find the total number of hectares burned, we need to calculate the area burned in two distinct phases and then sum the results.

### Step 1: Calculate the area burned in the first phase
*   **Rate**: $12$ hectares/hour
*   **Time**: $3.5$ hours
*   **Calculation**:
    $$ \text{Area}_1 = \text{Rate}_1 \times \text{Time}_1 $$
    $$ \text{Area}_1 = 12 \times 3.5 $$

    We can break down $3.5$ as $3 + 0.5$:
    $$ 12 \times 3 = 36 $$
    $$ 12 \times 0.5 = 6 $$
    $$ 36 + 6 = 42 $$

    So, the first phase resulted in **42 hectares** burned.

### Step 2: Calculate the rate and area burned in the second phase
*   **Updated Rate**: The rate doubled from Phase 1.
    $$ \text{Rate}_2 = 2 \times 12 = 24 \text{ hectares/hour} $$
*   **Time**: $2$ hours
*   **Calculation**:
    $$ \text{Area}_2 = \text{Rate}_2 \times \text{Time}_2 $$
    $$ \text{Area}_2 = 24 \times 2 $$
    $$ \text{Area}_2 = 48 $$

    So, the second phase resulted in **48 hectares** burned.

### Step 3: Calculate the total area burned
*   **Total Area**: $\text{Area}_1 + \text{Area}_2$
    $$ \text{Total Area} = 42 + 48 $$
    $$ \text{Total Area} = 90 $$

### Final Conclusion
The total number of hectares burned is **90**.
