1. **Ground fire**: Burns organic matter beneath the forest floor, such as roots and peat, often smoldering slowly and persistently.  
2. **Surface fire**: Consumes low-lying vegetation like grass, shrubs, and leaf litter, moving across the forest floor at a relatively moderate pace.  
3. **Crown fire**: Spreads rapidly through the tops of trees and shrubs, often driven by wind and producing intense heat, making it the most dangerous and destructive type of forest fire.
