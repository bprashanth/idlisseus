We are given that a forest patch burned at two different rates over two time periods.

---

**First phase:**
- Rate = 12 hectares per hour
- Time = 3.5 hours

So, area burned in first phase:
$$
12 \, \text{ha/hr} \times 3.5 \, \text{hr} = 42 \, \text{hectares}
$$

---

**Second phase:**
- The rate **doubled**, so new rate = $ 12 \times 2 = 24 $ hectares per hour
- Time = 2 hours

Area burned in second phase:
$$
24 \, \text{ha/hr} \times 2 \, \text{hr} = 48 \, \text{hectares}
$$

---

**Total area burned:**
$$
42 \, \text{ha} + 48 \, \text{ha} = 90 \, \text{hectares}
$$

---

✅ **Answer: $\boxed{90}$ hectares**
